## Official registration route

Open [myPATH](https://mypath.pa.gov/) through [PA’s Register My Business for Taxes page](https://www.pa.gov/services/revenue/register-my-business-for-taxes).

- **No existing PA tax accounts:** use **Registration → Pennsylvania Online Business Tax Registration → Business Tax Registration**.
- **Existing PA tax accounts:** sign in as the appropriate Primary Administrator. Select the correct taxpayer/business, then **More… → Accounts → Register New Business Tax Accounts → Business Tax Registration**. Existing personal-income-tax accounts can affect which route applies.
- Work through the questions and select the applicable **Sales, Use and Hotel Occupancy Retail Tax License** for taxable retail sales. Its combined name does not mean this candle shop operates a hotel. Add unrelated employer or other tax accounts only if its actual activities require them.
- Review before submitting; the portal also offers a draft that must be completed within 30 days. Save the confirmation privately and follow PA’s processing instructions.

Screen names and existing-account distinction: [PA Revenue registration instructions, updated March 3, 2026](https://revenue-pa.custhelp.com/app/answers/detail/a_id/4152/).

## After registration

Confirm the account is active, its effective date, filing frequency and first due date in myPATH. PA says new sales-tax accounts generally start quarterly, but existing-operation rules and later changes can affect the schedule; use the actual assigned account schedule. Set reminders for each filing and payment. [PA filing-frequency explanation](https://revenue-pa.custhelp.com/app/answers/detail/a_id/215/).

File required returns even for a period with no taxable transactions. Keep sales, tax, shipping, refunds and payment-provider fees separately recorded; a PayPal payout is not the same as gross sales. [PA sales-tax account and filing requirements](https://www.pa.gov/agencies/revenue/resources/tax-types-and-information/sales-use-and-hotel-occupancy-tax).

## Tax decisions to bring back to the store developer

**Candles and destination:** PA’s current guidance uses delivery destination for local sales tax. The statewide rate is 6%; taxable deliveries to Allegheny County total 7%, and Philadelphia total 8%. Do not use an older guide’s seller-location rule. A ZIP alone may not establish the delivery county. [Current PA local-sales-tax guidance](https://www.pa.gov/agencies/revenue/resources/tax-types-and-information/sales-use-and-hotel-occupancy-tax/local-sales-tax).

**Shipping:** confirm tax treatment of shipping on taxable candle orders; PA’s examples include shipping/handling in the taxable purchase amount. The store’s PA candle adapter currently includes shipping in its tax calculation. [PA purchase and shipping examples](https://www.pa.gov/agencies/revenue/resources/tax-types-and-information/sales-use-and-hotel-occupancy-tax/use-tax/use-tax-for-individuals).

**Direct store:** do not assume PayPal handles sales-tax registration, calculation or remittance for this custom website. PA specifically describes collection duties for direct online sales by businesses with inventory in Pennsylvania. Review other states separately based on the business’s actual obligations; do not assume every destination is taxable or exempt. [PA online-retailer guidance](https://www.pa.gov/agencies/revenue/resources/tax-types-and-information/tax-obligations-for-online-retailers).

**Tarot bundle — unresolved:** ask PA Revenue how this particular $11.11 recurring bundle is classified: one human-provided reading plus access to an interactive digital deck. Ask whether bundling changes taxability, whether separate pricing/allocation is needed, how delivery location is determined, and whether the advertised $11.11 includes or excludes any tax. Do not infer the entire bundle is exempt because part is a personal service. Use the [official Revenue customer-service portal](https://revenue-pa.custhelp.com/) for a written answer if the published guidance does not resolve it.
