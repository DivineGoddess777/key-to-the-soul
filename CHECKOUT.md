# Customer checkout

Implemented October 18, 2026. Live ordering and paid tarot subscriptions remain closed.

The ritual bag links to `/checkout.html` only when `/api/checkout/config` reports enabled checkout and every item has a size. A guest enters contact and delivery details, reviews a server-calculated quote, acknowledges shipping/return terms and continues to PayPal. No store account is required for candle checkout.

The server reserves confirmed stock, stores immutable catalog-priced order details, and creates a PayPal order with the same amount and provided delivery address. PayPal is instructed to keep that address fixed. Return URLs use the configured site origin. The receipt verifies PayPal's completed capture, currency, amount and local order ID before stock is committed. Refreshing the order can reconcile a lost capture response. Another browser session cannot retrieve the order by knowing its ID.

Duplicate attempts reuse the same local order and PayPal idempotency key. Database leases prevent simultaneous payment attempts or cancellation during capture. Late captures become PAYMENT_REVIEW and do not take stock reserved by another customer. Reservation expiry cleanup runs before new holds, is available in the owner desk, and is now included in the authenticated five-minute worker. It preserves unexpired holds, active payment leases and other environments; repeated cleanup does not release the same hold twice.

Confirmed shipping is $8.95 per order in the contiguous United States, free at $60 and up in candle subtotal before tax. Ship-from state/ZIP is PA 16335. Alaska, Hawaii and U.S. territories need calculated shipping rates, which are not yet connected; those destinations cannot use the flat-rate checkout. International shipping is excluded. Handling is 3–5 business days, shipping twice weekly by USPS Ground Advantage with tracking and estimated 2–5 business days transit. Damaged-transit candles are replaced or refunded; used/burned returns are excluded. Candles are made to order; per-variant availability and tax treatment remain to configure.

## Configuration gates

- Sandbox checkout requires Vercel Preview, KTTS_COMMERCE_SANDBOX=1, Preview database and sandbox app credentials. The legacy KTTS_PAYPAL_SANDBOX flag alone does not enable customer checkout.
- Live checkout requires KTTS_PRODUCTION=1, KTTS_LIVE_CHECKOUT=1, separate live credentials and reviewed fulfillment configuration. Production credentials are configured, but the payment flag remains 0 and final availability/fulfillment decisions are pending.
- KTTS_FULFILLMENT_JSON must explicitly provide reviewed=true, shipFromState, shipFromPostalCode, shippingRegions, confirmedNoTaxRegions and taxDecisionReference. PA candle collection uses taxMode=PA_DESTINATION with the declared delivery county; other states need explicit collection decisions. Do not copy synthetic QA decisions into Production. Absent tax setup fails closed.
- Each candle/size needs explicit ready stock or made-to-order availability in the owner desk.
- `/api/paypal-webhook` requires the appropriate private PAYPAL_SANDBOX_WEBHOOK_ID or PAYPAL_LIVE_WEBHOOK_ID. It verifies signatures and reconciles capture completion, refunds and reversals through fresh PayPal reads. Genuine Sandbox completion/refund delivery passed; the temporary Preview callback token was revoked afterward. The Live callback is registered and unsigned Production events are rejected; no genuine Live payment event has yet been created.

## Refunds, receipts and tax adapter — implementation update

Owner refunds use durable attempts, fixed PayPal idempotency keys, an exclusive lease and a confirmed amount. A lost response reuses the original attempt; a second open refund cannot silently change the amount. Completed/partial refunds and signed reversals reconcile through fresh PayPal capture/refund reads. Stale completion events do not restore a reversed payment. Unfulfilled adjusted orders need owner review; inventory is never automatically restored. Synthetic coverage is supplemented by a genuine hosted full Sandbox refund and processed signed refund callback.

The owner desk has refund confirmation and retry controls. Customer receipts display payment adjustments. A separate HMAC-signed 30-day receipt link allows read-only receipt access on another device; it cannot initiate payment or refunds. Invalid/expired/environment-mismatched links do not open storage. The checkout document uses no-referrer policy for these private links.

A transactional outbox queues paid-order, shipment and payment-adjustment email with the order update. Google Workspace SMTP uses TLS on port 465, fixed support aliases, no remote attachments and redacted errors. `KTTS_STORE_EMAIL=1`, private `KTTS_SMTP_USERNAME`, `KTTS_SMTP_PASSWORD`, `KTTS_RECEIPT_SECRET` and a verified scheduled maintenance worker are required before live checkout. The approved credential is now sensitive and Production-only as well as separately configured in Preview. One labeled Sandbox payment-update receipt was accepted, recorded as sent and confirmed received; the real owner’s Production recovery email was subsequently delivered and confirmed too. The authenticated Production worker runs every five minutes. See EMAIL-SETUP.md, qa/workspace-preview-test-receipt.json and qa/keianna-production-recovery-confirmed.json. A stable Message-ID and leases reduce duplicates, but SMTP plus a database cannot promise exactly-once delivery after an acknowledgment is lost. Failures remain queued with backoff and a finite review threshold.

`taxMode=PA_DESTINATION` supports Pennsylvania candle sales from the declared delivery county: 6% statewide, 7% Allegheny and 8% Philadelphia, including taxable shipping. This follows the official destination rule currently effective in 2026: https://www.pa.gov/agencies/revenue/resources/tax-types-and-information/sales-use-and-hotel-occupancy-tax/local-sales-tax . County is a customer-supplied part of the delivery address; this is not address geocoding. Other enabled states still require explicit collection decisions. A PA-origin store cannot set PA destinations to zero merely by putting PA in confirmedNoTaxRegions. Missing decisions remain closed; the app does not request a general business-license document.

Live checkout additionally requires configured live webhook ID, private maintenance bearer and enabled Workspace mail. The completed USD 22.21 hosted candle test covered shipping and calculated tax; the earlier USD 12 proof belongs to the legacy adapter.
